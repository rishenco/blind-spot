# 2025–26 Market — Research Brief

## TL;DR

- **2025 broke the record for indie breakouts**: 8 indie titles passed 100K peak CCU (previous record: 7, in 2016). R.E.P.O. (18.4M units), PEAK (15.7M), Schedule I (8M) sold ~42M copies and grossed ~$377.5M on Steam alone, all from teams of 1–7 people ([VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/), [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/megabonk-eighth-indie-to-hit-over-100000-steam-players-this-year/)).
- **2026's defining hit is asymmetric multiplayer PvP, not co-op**: MECCHA CHAMELEON (paint-yourself-into-the-wall hide-and-seek, $5, 1–2 devs) hit **340,534 peak CCU** and 20M+ copies by Aug 2026 — the fastest-selling game of 2026, with zero marketing budget ([SteamDB](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/SteamDB/announcements/detail/671745681362257533), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meccha_Chameleon)). The single most relevant precedent for Blind Spot.
- **Small-format ranked PvP (1v1/2v2) is close to a confirmed dead zone.** Duel Corp. — a 1v1/2v2 indie at $24.99, EA Jan 2026, *Mostly Positive* — went from 2,485 peak CCU to **8** in five months ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/duel-corp/steam-charts)). Quality was not the failure mode; population was.
- **Co-op decays gracefully; PvP decays to zero.** R.E.P.O. retains ~29K peak CCU 18 months post-launch (11% of peak). Rematch fell 92,841 → <5,000 (~5%). Duel Corp: 0.3%. Co-op lobbies are self-supplied; PvP lobbies are not.
- **Price is the acquisition lever.** Every 2026 volume breakout is $5–12. Above $10, median wishlist→sales conversion halves (0.15x → 0.10x) ([VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/)).
- **Co-op horror is saturated as a category but not as a mechanic space** — the survivors all carry one structural twist (R.E.P.O. physics, PEAK verticality). Echolocation-as-perception is a *rendering* twist, which is meaningfully harder to clone than a content twist.
- **The math is brutal**: 20,282 Steam releases in 2025, only 608 (2.99%) reached 1,000 reviews; ~300 games earned >$1M.
- **Biggest no-go signal is scope, not concept**: four modes = four populations and four balance problems, built against competitors who ship in one month.

### Q2 — Patterns: saturated vs fresh (as of Aug 2026)

**Still working:** trivial price ($5–12) as an impulse-purchase engine; one-sentence hook; streamer-legible *systemic* loops (not scripted ones); demo-driven launches (Cairn's 500K demo → 300K sales); co-op roguelite structure (LORT proves it charts).

**Saturated:** generic "friendslop" co-op horror with proximity chat. Reporting is explicit that the market is "as saturated as ever" ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/3d/video-game-design/what-is-friendslop-and-why-it-it-taking-over-gaming-in-2026)), and Lethal Company derivatives without a twist are no longer charting. Hide-and-seek is now *actively cloning* — Fake Me, AI-generated Scribble Hunt, and Roblox knock-offs that [reportedly out-player the original](https://kotaku.com/more-people-are-playing-meccha-chameleon-knock-offs-than-the-original-hit-steam-game-as-the-roblox-clone-machine-goes-into-overdrive-2000715371). Clone latency for an asset-swappable mechanic is now **weeks**.

**Fresh:** perception/sensory mechanics (unclonable without engine work); slow-burn psychological horror displacing jump-scare templates; first-person simulation microgenres.

### Q3 — Small-format PvP viability

Three data points, all pointing one way:

1. **Spectre Divide** — ~$60–65M invested, 5 years, ~400K players week one, 30K+ peak CCU, never above 4,000 afterward. Shut down and studio closed within **six months** of launch, Mar 2025 ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/mountaintop-studios-shutting-down-after-debut-shooter-spectre-divide-falls-short)).
2. **Rematch** — 92,841 peak → sub-5,000, **-90%+**; blamed on netcode, slow patch cadence, and Sloclap lacking live-service staffing depth ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/rematch-loses-95-percent-of-players-since-launch/)).
3. **Duel Corp.** — the closest analogue to Blind Spot's 1v1/2v2 pitch. $24.99, 1,247 reviews *Mostly Positive*, 2,485 → **8** CCU.

**Condition under which indie PvP survives:** when the match format tolerates a wide skill spread and a small pool — i.e. **party-asymmetric, 6–12 players, no ranked ladder, no ELO expectation**. MECCHA CHAMELEON is PvP and it is the biggest game of 2026. Identity V (4v1) persists. What dies is *symmetric, small-N, skill-ranked* PvP, because it needs a deep matched pool per skill bracket per region — the exact thing an indie population cannot supply.

### Q4 — Fit

The concept sits at the intersection of two proven 2026 shapes: **asymmetric hide-and-seek PvP** (the year's #1 hit) and **co-op roguelite** (LORT, still charting). The echolocation layer is genuinely underserved — the space is essentially game-jam scale plus Muffled Warfare. No commercial-scale echolocation multiplayer game exists. That's opportunity **and** an absence of proof-of-demand.

Underserved niche: **a perception-asymmetry party game** — one side sees, one side doesn't; or both are blind and audio is the shared battlefield. That is Blind Spot's flag-hunt mode with the PvP framing inverted onto it.

### Q5 — Marketing reality

- **Wishlists**: 5,000 is the floor, **7,000** is breakeven and the Popular Upcoming / Next Fest Gold cutoff, 50,000 signals a strong launch ([presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-many-wishlists-to-launch), [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-many-wishlists-before-launch)).
- **Conversion**: median 0.15x wishlist→first-week sales at 25K+ WL. Viral outliers break the model entirely — **PEAK converted at 29.29x, R.E.P.O. at 7.51x**. Wishlists predict *floor*, not ceiling.
- **Next Fest is not a plan**: June 2026 had 4,200+ demos. Median Feb 2026 demo gained ~200 wishlists; top 5% ~7,000. You need **2,000 WL before entering**, and a demo shipped *well before* the fest earns ~2.5x the wishlists of one shipped during it.
- **Streamers**: genre-fit micro-creators beat mega-creators (+36% vs **-17%** engagement effect). Seed 50–100 genre creators in staggered waves with unique keys and UTMs, not one blast.
- Successful indies averaged **91% review score** and **214 days** of pre-launch visibility.

### Q6 — No-go signals

1. **Mode sprawl.** Solo training + 1v1 + 2v2 + co-op roguelike = four populations to fill and four balance surfaces. Nothing in the 2025–26 data rewards this; everything rewards one mode executed hard.
2. **Price/format mismatch.** A $20+ PvP-forward game is precisely Duel Corp's profile.
3. **Onboarding cost.** Friendslop wins because time-to-first-laugh is ~60 seconds. Echolocation + parkour + energy management + skill tree is a genuinely hard first ten minutes. The concept's most underrated risk.
4. **Scope asymmetry.** PEAK: 7 people, one month, <$200K. Meccha Chameleon: 1–2 people, UEFN prototype, asset store. A bespoke point-cloud renderer + rollback-quality netcode + parkour + roguelike meta is a 2–3 year build competing for the same shelf.

## What this means for Blind Spot

1. **Kill 1v1/2v2 as a launch pillar.** Not "deprioritize" — cut. The evidence is unambiguous and the format is the single highest-mortality shape in indie multiplayer. If competitive is wanted later, ship it as an unranked side mode after the population exists.
2. **Make the flagship mode asymmetric, 6–8 players, PvP-flavoured but party-framed.** The hidden-flag hunt is already 80% of the way to the 2026 winning shape. One team is blind and hunting; the hider has different senses. That reads as one sentence, it is instantly clippable, and it inherits Meccha Chameleon's proven demand without being its clone.
3. **Price $8–12, premium, no live service.** Every volume breakout of 2026 lives here, and conversion economics collapse above $10 for impulse buys.
4. **The echolocation renderer is the moat — lean into it visually.** Point-cloud reveal is instantly recognizable in a thumbnail — passes the one-screenshot test better than almost anything in the friendslop cohort. Crucially, it is *not* asset-swappable: a Roblox clone farm cannot reproduce it in three weeks the way it reproduced Meccha Chameleon.
5. **Robots should be goofy, not cold.** R.E.P.O. is literally robots and it works because the physics make them stupid. Tense + funny is achievable; tense + sleek sci-fi is a much smaller audience.
6. **Roguelike/skill tree/augmentations are retention, not acquisition.** Do not lead marketing with them, and do not build them before the core loop is proven in playtests.
7. **Solo training mode earns its keep** — but as an anti-death-spiral hedge (playable at 3am with zero players online), not as a mode you market.

## Confidence & gaps

**High confidence:** Spectre Divide, Rematch, Duel Corp trajectories; PEAK team/budget/price; Schedule I and R.E.P.O. peaks and revenue; Steam release volume and the 2.99% figure; wishlist benchmarks and Next Fest medians.

**Medium:** MECCHA CHAMELEON's exact figures — sources disagree (13M vs 15M vs 20M+ units; one dev vs two; $4.79 vs $5–6). Directionally certain, precisely uncertain. Megabonk's peak CCU is reported as both 88,342 and 117,000. R.E.P.O.'s current CCU comes from third-party trackers with ±20% spread.

**Gaps:** no dev postmortem exists for a commercially-scaled echolocation multiplayer game — the niche has no failure data *or* success data, which cuts both ways. No hard numbers on parkour/movement-focused indie multiplayer as a category. "Far Far West" (8 devs, ~2K sustained CCU) appeared in one aggregated summary, unverified. Two data-dense sources (games-stats.com Q1 2026 "Indie-pocalypse", Shahrabi's 2026 deep dive) returned 403 and are worth a manual read.
